### What an exact transcription must keep

- Energy drain (&0204E-&02062): d1 = speed asr.w #4, then andi.w #$1FF, so the shift is arithmetic and the mask comes after it. sub.w with bcs: only a borrow ends the game, setting energy 0 and $12C=1. An exact 0 is stored and play goes on; at speeds below 16 the drain is 0, so energy can sit at 0 for ever.
- Warnings (&01F40, &01F82): cmpi.w #$2EE0 then bcc, an unsigned compare of shields and energy.
- Ground scrape (&02082): cmpi.w #$FFEC,$130(a6); blt is signed, so it scrapes when y >= -20. player_flight (&078FE) clamps y to at most -20, so scraping means sitting at the clamp. subi.w #$12C with bcs: a borrow ends the game (shields 0, $12C=2).
- Ram damage (&02124-&0213C): asl.w #2 of the level indexes the 16-long table &0AE1E without bounds. sub.w with bcc: a result of exactly 0 carries on and is caught next frame by tst.w $260 at &01FC4. In cheat mode the refill at &02036 comes after the damage, so a borrow still ends the game.
- Measured in Hatari: ramming a type-4 object with $296 forced to 16 cost &916E (37,230). The read at &2C0134 (24-bit &012C012C+8, unmapped on 1 MB) returned the prefetched opcode of the next instruction, sub.w d0,$260(a6) = &916E. Level 31 cost &00E0, the word at &0008 (TOS's bus-error vector high word). Level 32 raised a bus error at &500058 (PC &12AF4), so the game stops. So rams from level 16 (150,000 points) on depend on the machine, and from level 32 (310,000) on they crash it.
- The shields-zero test is tst.w $260 == 0 (&01FC4). Enemy fire (&07E66) and walker rams (&08510) clamp at 0 rather than borrow, so this test is what catches them.
- Roll jolt (&02116-&0211E): (random8 & &3E) - &20 is even, -32..+30, added with add.w (wraps). player_flight moves it 2 toward 0 each frame, so it stays even.
- Every game-over path branches to frame_loop. One more full frame (render and all logic) runs before &01F38 sees $12C.
- On a frame where $2D7 is set, &01F30 jumps to level_start before any of the post-frame tests: no warning, drain, scrape, ram damage or cheat refill that frame.
- sub_026B2 calls frame_end, and sub_039B2 calls it once at a new game. Each adds 1 to $1E2, clears $242 and swaps the buffers. A level change therefore advances the frame count once more than the frames run.
- init_objects: mulu.w #$290 of a word sector keeps the low word (99*656 = &FDB0 fits). Group offsets use addi.w/subi.w and wrap on the 16-bit torus. $1D8 = d7 + 10. Clearing uses dbra from &8B (140 records).
- random8 (&046B2) adds the caller's d0.w plus D_01842 to the word at $5C(a6) before stepping, and returns the word $21A. The incoming d0 matters. In this range: layout entries call with d0 = their type (2, 14, 5, 17, 3); the first tunnel-object call with d0 = 0 (the layout's terminating word) and each later one with the previous result; ram_damage with d0 = type*2. The X flag inside random8 is set by its own add.w at &046BA.
- Byte tests treat any non-zero as true: $2D7, $2BF, $2C8, $2CA, $2C3, $2CD, object +02 and +24 (tst.b). The +02 test at &020E2 is == 0, not bit 7: enemy missiles (+02 = 1) die by ramming but score nothing.
- Waits compare signed differences (cmp.w &1E4,d7; bpl at &01ECE and &021C0), so they are safe across the VBL count's wrap.
- &0209E-&020B6 is a no-op except for leaving d0 = $1E2 & &7FF (or & &3FF) at frame_loop. After a level change d0 enters frame_loop holding the last random8 result (measured &4B6B on level 1).

### Random numbers and timing

- random8 &046B2 is called at &02112: once per ram outside missile view, unless the object is a silo.
- init_objects calls random8 through sub_0A88E once per layout entry of type 2, 14, 5, 17 or 3, then five times for &1218/&1258. Per level: 1: 38+5=43; 2: 34+5=39; 3: 34+5=39; 4+: 36+5=41. Those for &1218/&1258 only advance the state, because docking overwrites the positions.
- random8 reads the mouse x accumulator D_01842, which the ACIA interrupt (&00C32) changes whenever a mouse packet arrives, even between two random8 calls in one frame. A capture must hold the mouse still within a frame, or record when packets arrive.
- The new-game set-up (sub_039FA, outside the range) calls sub_04750 (82 random8 calls) before it clears D_01842 at &03A86. So the title-screen mouse position feeds the whole game's random sequence unless the mouse is untouched. Measured with no mouse movement: seed $5C-$61 = 13 6A 57 D4 2B 15 (X=0 entering the addx at &0466C); after the level-1 init, 6B 6B 4B B4 89 7E with $64 = &490DCD88.
- $1E2 (frame count) is read at &01F50 and &01F92 (speech every 128 frames) and at &0209E (dead code). It is never reset per game, so its value at game start depends on how many title passes ran (4, plus 2 per title return; 6 measured at 38 s, 8 at the first frame_loop). Capture it.
- &1E4 (VBL count) is read only by the title wait, the game-over wait and the level flash's fade (wait_vbl). No logic state depends on it.
- D_01E56 (sample bytes left, counted down by the Timer A interrupt at 6144 or 9830 Hz) gates the speech warnings and sfx_start (&0209A effect 3, &020C4 effect 1); sfx_start also tests &29C, the VBL-counted lockout. Leave all of it out of the comparison.
- Step boundary: the post-frame logic (&01F30-&020B6) runs after frame_end, and $242 and $D4 carry over it. A step taken from frame_loop (&01F10) to the next frame_loop includes that logic. A level change re-enters through &01F06 and init_objects and still reaches &01F10.
- The level flash's 65,536-pass colour-15 loop and its 5-VBL fade are wall-clock time only.

### What it reads of the renderer's

- $2BF and $AC come from collisions (&08416), which walks the near list ($98, count $240) and reads the camera-space +28/+2A/+2C that world_render's lists (&0561C, &057B8) computed. So the ram damage here depends on the renderer's camera-relative positions.
- $292 (dock_state), tested at &01FCC and &02028, is written in sub_0561C, the list routine inside world_render: the tunnel's end test sets &1E at &056A2 from (silo +08 - player z) < &C8, and the launch countdown decrements it at &05652. That is game logic running inside the renderer.
- $2D7, $296 and $298 (the level change) are written in world_render &0502C-&0505A from the score's second BCD byte (&0150D) against $2D5. The score add ($D4, written here) happens after that check (&05088-&0509E), so a kill raises the level two frames later.
- $242 written here is used by frame_end; $D4 by world_render.
- 0(a6) and 4(a6), the screen pointers swap_screens maintains, are where the post-frame and level texts go.
- Nothing in the range reads the camera matrix, projected coordinates or the draw list.

### Inputs

- D_01852 (last key, a scancode; text-relative, Hatari &109BE+&01852). On the title and the game-over wait, sub_049AE (&01EC6, &021B8) returns ASCII D_015F6[scancode-1] and clears the byte. Keys that map to 0 (cursor keys, the mouse buttons as &74/&75) are ignored, so they don't start the game. The title masks with &DF: 'G' needs cheat mode, 'K' opens the K screen, any other key starts. The K screen waits in sub_049A4 for the raw scancode with no timeout and no clear; sub_00BCA returns ASCII with a 1500-VBL timeout that returns &0D. D_01852 is cleared at &01EA2, &02204, &0223E and &02362.
- Defined keys: the K screen stores raw scancodes in $2D8-$2DD (0 at boot), which read_keys uses as indices into the key-state array.
- Mouse: D_01842 is read only through random8.
- The key-state array &017C2 is not read in the range. sub_00A9E's start-up clear covers only &017C2-&017C5 (bge at &00AC2).
- No joystick is read.
- ESC is handled in read_keys (&0271A, then &03FF2): it pops the return address and jumps to the title (&01E94), with no game-over screen or high score.

- 'The frame (&01F10)': the code after frame_end (&01F30-&020B6) is not just warnings and tests. ### What an exact transcription must keep

- Key tests are tst.b != 0. A defined key indexes key_state as an unsigned byte (clr.w d0; move.b), so an index of &80 or more would read &01842 and beyond.
- Accelerate and brake are one exclusive chain (space, then '/', then [$2DD] brake, then [$2DC] accelerate). The two bank tests (left, then right) and the two climb tests (up, then down) are separate, so pressing both cancels.
- The adds to &01842/&01844/&01848 (subi.w/addi.w on memory) wrap at 16 bits and happen before player_flight's clamps. The ACIA's add.w of the sign-extended byte can arrive between any two instructions.
- Command keys come from the single latch &01852 through &015F6, compared with &7F and &1B before `andi.w #$DF`. Every command except I (and I when not docked) returns at once, so a command costs that frame's flight keys.
- Silo launch: an exact word compare $292 == &1E, then subq to &1D. The two random32 results are discarded, but the state ($64) advances.
- The missile cap is `cmpi.w #2; beq` (equality), checked before the cheat-mode path, so $278 never exceeds 2.
- The cheat needs `tst.w &01848` == 0 exactly and $2C1 != 0. $294 resets to 0 on every second success, whether or not those hold. A wrong key at sequence position k swallows 7-k further keys.
- random32: `add.l d0,d0; bhi skip; eori.l #$1D872B41` (EOR when C=1 or the result is 0); `add.w &01842` affects only the low word (no carry into the high word); `add.l $5C(a6)`; swap.
- The Interrogate approach is `cmpi.w #$400,$198; bmi` (the N flag of the difference): from &1E00 it steps -&64 down to &3D4 in 67 frames. Heading += &40 a frame, wrapping at 16 bits (the matrix masks it to 12 bits).
- score_add_print: four abcd from the least significant byte, starting with X = 0. That X comes from the printer called just before at &05082: its last X-affecting instruction is `subi.b #$20` on a hex digit of $286, which does not borrow. CLR, MOVE, CMP, ANDI and ROL leave X alone. The carry out of the top byte is lost, so the score wraps at 99,999,999. The addend is $D4 zero-extended (clr.l d0; move.w), at most 4 BCD digits.
- ACIA: a byte is a header iff it is >= &F8 unsigned (d0 zero-extended). A break clears key_state[byte & &7F]; a make sets key_state[byte] = 1 and &01852 = byte. dx and dy are ext.w then add.w.
- wait_key_timeout: d1 = VBL + &5DC; it loops while the N flag of `cmp.w &1E4,d1` is clear, so it times out after 1501 VBLs and returns &0D.

### Random numbers and timing

- random32 (&04692) is called twice in silo_launch (&0284A, &0284E). It mixes in &01842 at the time of the call, which the ACIA changes asynchronously, and $5C.l. No other random call is in the range.
- The VBL count &1E4 is read only by wait_key_timeout during the cheat sequence, for 1501-VBL timeouts. wait_key (&00C0C) blocks with no timeout after Backspace or Delete. The ST version has no other pause key.
- Interrogate runs its own frames inside read_keys. Each calls animate_shapes (the 7 frame lists advance) and frame_end ($1E2 += 1, $242 written to colour 0 and cleared, screens swapped), with no world_render, handlers or collisions. An Interrogate session is therefore N extra steps that change only $1E2, the animation counters, $242 and the draw scratch. The scene capture's breakpoint at &05108 (world_render) never fires during them; the frame_end breakpoint &08EC2 would.
- Inputs are asynchronous. Key and mouse bytes land at any instruction. The step reads them at player_laser (&08684, key_state), read_keys (&01852, key_state, the accumulators), player_flight (the accumulators) and random32 or random8 (&01842) in the handlers, so a byte between two of these reads makes them disagree within one frame.
- Sound is gated by $2CB, &01E56 and &29C (a VBL countdown, in sfx_start). Sound changes no logic state, but Timer A load changes how many VBLs a frame takes, and so where inputs land.

### What it reads of the renderer's

- read_keys reads none of world_render's results: not the coarse, near or draw lists, the camera-space positions, the matrices or the projected coordinates.
- Interrogate's frames call draw_shape themselves, with $1F8, $17A-$17E and $194-$198. That overwrites the renderer's scratch: the matrix $156-$166, the angle cache $180-$184, $1DA-$1E0, $10-$28, $84-$8C and $1EC-$1F0. world_render rewrites it all for each object, so the next frame is unaffected.
- Interrogate's panel call (&05872, which includes the scanner) reads the stale near list $98/$240 and each listed object's +00, +28 and +2C. This is display only.
- score_add_print reads 0(a6) (the draw buffer) and $128(a6). The model viewer uses draw_shape the same way, on the title only.

### Inputs

- Key state: flight_keys reads key_state &017C2 slots &39 (space), &35 '/', &4B/&4D (cursor left/right), &2D/&2E (X/C), &48/&50 (cursor up/down), &27 ';', &34 '.', and $2D8-$2DD (the defined keys, 0 unless K was used [measured]).
- Last key: read_keys (once per frame), Interrogate (once per Interrogate frame), the cheat (blocking) and the viewer read &01852 through &015F6. The commands are Esc (&1B, to the title with no end screen); Backspace or Delete (&7F, the cheat prompt); and F, M, L, P, Q, S, I, N. Interrogate accepts only &20 and 'E' (uppercase from the table), so the right mouse button (&75, read as &20 through &0166A) also advances it [inferred from the table; the &75 make is measured].
- Mouse: the IKBD is set up with threshold 1,1 and buttons as keys (command 7, 4). In Hatari [measured, a breakpoint at &115FC]: a right click sends &75, then the header &F9 (bit 0) with dx and dy (which go through &00D16 and &00D40, so dy reaches &01848); the release sends &F5, then &F8 0 0. A left click sends &74, then &FA (bit 1): &0184A and &0184C become &FF and key_state[&74] = 1. Hatari's IKBD keeps the button bits in relative packets even in buttons-as-keys mode (src/ikbd.c, IKBD_SendRelMousePacket). Moving the mouse right adds to &01842 (a right turn); moving it towards you adds to &01844 (a climb); with the right button held, pushing it away subtracts from &01848 (faster).
- Joystick: none. No IKBD command disables joystick reporting, yet the handler parses &FE/&FF packets as mouse headers (left bit set; &FF's bit 0 sends the following byte to &01848). A joystick event would desynchronise the parser. Hatari enables joystick 1 by default only when the host has a real joystick (configuration.c).
- The ACIA handler reads $FFFC02 without checking the status register, and takes &F6 and &F7 (IKBD status and absolute packets) as releases of &76 and &77.

### What an exact transcription must keep

- Velocity: muls.w speed x S into 32 bits, asl.l #1, swap. The word is floor(p/32768), an arithmetic floor (a small negative product gives -1, not 0). Same for the missile with 400.
- Order within a frame: the move uses $21E and $13C as the previous frame left them. Speed, heading and y are updated afterwards, and y's climb is this frame's $23A.
- Every shift is asr (floor): roll = -(mx asr 4), so mx = -1 gives roll +1; climb = -(d asr 4); pitch = my asr 1; sight x = mx asr 2; sight y = (-my) asr 3, negated before the shift, not -(my asr 3). Mono uses asr 1 and asr 2.
- All the clamps are signed word compares (ble, bge, bgt, blt) and are written back to the accumulators. mx: > &1F0 to &1F0, < -&1F0 to -&1F0. my: +-&140. D_01848: <= -479 to -479, else > 0 to 0. Only the climb has the dead zone (\|my\| <= 48 gives 0); the pitch uses the raw, adjusted my.
- Altitude: y >= -20 (equal included, every frame) gives y = -20 and my += 8. Else y < -1800 gives my -= 8 and y = -1800. The pitch is taken after this, from the adjusted my, which can reach +-328.
- The heading is never masked when stored: a full 16-bit wrapping word. Positions wrap at 16 bits.
- Roll jolt: roll += $280, then tst/bpl: negative +2, positive -2, zero untouched. The jolts are even ((r & &3E) - &20, in [-32, 30]) and the variable starts at 0, so it always settles at 0. An odd value would oscillate forever.
- Missile ramp: cmp.w $27A,d0 / bpl tests the N flag of the 16-bit difference, not a signed >=.
- Missile ground guard: add.w climb to $26C, and keep it only if the 16-bit result is negative (bmi); otherwise subtract it back. That also blocks a wrap past -32768.
- subq #1/beq on $276: the view ends only on exactly 0; from 0 it would wrap to &FFFF.
- Docked, x and z still move by the speed (10 down the tunnel). Heading and y don't change; roll and pitch still follow the accumulators.
- random8: the X flag chains. The carry of the word add $5C:$5D += (d0_in + mx) feeds the first addx, and each addx's carry feeds the next through both rounds; the move.b's in between keep X. d0_in is the caller's d0.w, so the caller's register state is an input.
- random32: add.l d0,d0 then bhi. The EOR is applied when C = 1 or the result is zero (so a zero state recovers). mx is added to the low word only (add.w; no carry into the high word). The whole long $5C-$5F is added with add.l. The result is returned swapped: d0's high word holds the state's low word, and all the callers use only d0.w.
- sub_0465A's first addx takes the X flag from whatever ran before it. At game start that's the palette fade sub_06ACC, whose last add.w d1,(a0)+ adds 0 in its final pass, so X = 0 (measured: 13 6A 57 D4 2B 15).
- Animation index: cmp.w last / bne: equality wrap (cur == last gives 0, else +1). The beam colour: (c + 1) & 15; its 'skip 0' test fires only for c = &FFFF.
- dots_scatter writes 41 pairs into a 40-pair table; a byte-exact port keeps the 41st in $8AC-$8AF. dots_restore copies 40 pairs (its dbra jumps into sub_0477E's identical loop).

### Random numbers and timing

- player_flight, animate_shapes, dots_save and dots_restore call no random routine and read neither the VBL count (&1E4) nor the frame count ($1E2). animate_shapes steps once a call, so the animations run per rendered frame, not per VBL.
- dots_scatter calls random8 82 times (41 dots x 2). sub_039FA runs it twice at a new game: at &03A06, before D_01842 is cleared (&03A86) and before the player's position is set, so those 82 calls use whatever mouse x the previous game or the title left, and the old $12E/$132; then at &03B18 with mx = 0 and x = z = &8020. The two random32 calls at &03AE4/&03AF2 (results thrown away) come between.
- The seed is reset only at a new game (sub_0465A at &039FE), not at a level change (L_01F06 re-runs init_objects only). From boot, two games gave identical dots: the start-of-game random stream is fixed while D_01842 is 0 at the first dots_scatter [measured: run 2's dots equal run 1's computed ones].
- Both generators add D_01842 at every call. The ACIA interrupt changes D_01842 whenever a mouse packet arrives, even between two random calls in one frame. With keys only, it changes only in read_keys (+-&20), player_flight (the clamp), the new-game clear and the model viewer (&03218).
- random8's d0_in is a hidden input at every caller: the rammed type x 2 at &02112 (measured $30, a neutron missile), the previous dot word in dots_scatter, and init_objects' working values. At the dot recycler &0486C it was 7 in 184 of 203 calls (the projection shift $212 left by the last projected dot), otherwise the last recycled value or world_render's d0 [measured].
- Traced calls in 117 flight frames [measured]: random8 615 (recycler 406, dots_scatter 164, init_objects 43, ramming 2); random32 3,511, about 30 a frame, almost all from the handlers (&07C78 1,310, &07CBC 1,053, &08160 600, &0817C 232, &07C5E 131, &07C3A 80, &08222 77).
- missile_view_end calls sfx_start, which does nothing while D_01E56 is set, sound is off ($2CB), or the VBL-decremented countdown &29C is non-zero. Otherwise it writes $2D1 and starts the effect. A VBL-timed dependency, but only on sound state.

### What it reads of the renderer's

- player_flight, animate_shapes and the random routines read none of the coarse, near or draw lists, the camera matrix, camera-space positions or projected coordinates.
- random8's state is shared with world_render's ground-dot recycler (&047AA). It calls random8 twice for each dot that leaves the view (&0486C/&04876 in flight, &048E2/&048EC in the missile view). Whether a dot leaves depends on its camera-space position from sub_04092 (the camera matrix $168-$178) and on the test z <= 2 or \|x\|, \|y\| > 2z. The d0 it passes is a renderer register. random32 adds $5C-$5F in, so every handler's random numbers depend on the renderer's dot results: an exact step must run the dot recycler exactly, d0 included.
- world_render's launch countdown sub_0561C (&05652-&0565E) decrements $292, calls random32 (colour 0) and adds 5 to D_01848 each launch frame, before read_keys and player_flight run. The renderer changes logic state that player_flight reads.
- The ground-dot table $80C-$8AB belongs to the renderer (recycled every frame, but not while docked: &050F6), yet dots_scatter and dots_restore write it.
- Outputs the renderer consumes next frame: $12E-$132, $13A, $13C and $13E (the camera); $244/$246 (the sight and the laser's aim point); in the missile view $26A-$274; the shape table +6 offsets and &0D97E (which frame and colour draw_shape uses); &06011 (the scanner).

### Inputs

- The key-state array &017C2 and the last key &01852 aren't read in this range. Keys arrive through read_keys' changes to the accumulators: cursor left &4B / X &2D / defined key $2D8 subtract &20 from D_01842, and right &4D / C &2E / $2D9 add &20. Up &48 / ; &27 / $2DA subtract &20 from D_01844 (dive), and down &50 / . &34 / $2DB add &20. Space &39 / $2DC subtract &10 from D_01848 (faster), and / &35 / $2DD add &10. There's no auto-centring: the accumulators hold their values.
- D_01842 (turn): player_flight's &07800-&07828 clamp, roll and heading; place_sight &079CA/&079F6; random32 &046A0 and random8 &046B4.
- D_01844 (climb/pitch): &07832-&0786E clamp, dead zone and climb; &0790A/&07918 the altitude feedback; &07924 the pitch; &0795C the missile's pitch; &079DE/&07A0A the sight.
- D_01848 (speed): &0787A-&078C6, outside the missile view.
- The mouse buttons D_0184A/D_0184C and the joystick are read nowhere in this range. The ACIA handler sends every byte >= &F8 down the mouse path (&00C3E), so there's no joystick path.

### Corrections to st.md

- 'The shapes': the fourth offset in a shape-table entry (+C, stored in $1C(a6)) isn't an unused block. For the seven animated shapes it points at the shape's frame list (&0D6CE, &0C17E, &0D99C, &0FD4C, &10768, &11C18, &1204C); for the others, at a &FFFF word after the faces.
- 'A counter at &0D97E steps 0-15 into the code at &06011' is wrong on both counts. &0D97E is word +0 (the colour) of shape 11's only face, the energy beam's. &06011 is data, not code: entry 11 of the scanner's per-type colour bytes at &06006. So the beam and its scanner dot cycle through colours 0-15, one step a frame, and the beam vanishes one frame in 16 (colour 0 draws nothing). In the shape table row, 'colour 15' for shape 11 is only its initial value.
- Animation: the Walker list &0D6CE drives shapes 1 and 10 together. animate_shapes also runs in the Interrogate loop (&02B3A) and the model viewer (&0327C). The indices start at 0 from the file and are never reset, so their phase at any frame is the number of calls since boot [measured]. The scene captures need the list words themselves, because the ping-pong lists repeat offsets.
- Random numbers: random32 adds the mouse x into the low word only, adds the long at $5C-$5F, and returns the new state's high word (swapped). random8 adds the caller's d0 as well as mouse x into the word $5C. Both are re-seeded at each new game by sub_0465A (&039FE), from $218 = &1357 and &314159, to 13 6A 57 D4 2B 15 / 00314159 [measured]. The renderer's ground dots consume most of random8's calls, so the stream depends on the view as well as on steering.
- 'The player' / 'Coordinates': x += speed*S/32768 is a floor (asl.l #1, swap), uses the previous frame's speed and heading, and is skipped in the missile view. Speed runs 0-479 (D_01848 clamped to [-479, 0]), 10 down a silo's tunnel and 0 at its end. Heading += mx asr 4, stored unmasked. Climb = -(my asr 4) with a dead zone \|my\| <= 48. Pitch = my asr 1. Altitude is clamped to [-1800, -20], with +8 or -8 fed back into the climb accumulator at the limits, which lifts the nose at the ground. Roll = -(mx asr 4) plus a decaying jolt $280 from ramming and mines.
- Sight: y = 68 + ((-my) asr 3), negated before the shift, from my after the altitude feedback. Mono: 320 + (mx asr 1), 136 + ((-my) asr 2).
- Missile view (the research only says 'the view follows it'): the player freezes, apart from its roll. A 32-frame ramp of $274 from -512 to 0 comes first; then the missile flies at 400 on its own heading $272, steered by mx and climbing with the climb rate, kept below y = 0. It lasts 255 frames, or 16 more after a hit; at the end the ground dots are restored from $8AC and effect 2 plays.
- $140/$144 (the player's velocity) and $248/$24A (the sight's offsets) are written every frame and never read.
- dots_scatter makes 41 dots for a 40-dot table (the 41st lands in the backup at $8AC). At a new game its first call runs before D_01842 is cleared.

### What an exact transcription must keep

- Every position, heading and pitch update is add.w with 16-bit wrap. Headings are stored unmasked by the silo (+$10), the mines (-$40) and the wanderers (±16). turn_step (&07D32) and launches store them masked to 12 bits; readers mask with $FFF.
- The comparisons are cmp/cmpi followed by bpl/bmi: they test bit 15 of the 16-bit difference, not a signed less-than. This applies to the near test (dx/dz against $3E8 and $FC18), the mine zone (+2C against $F800, $800, $600), the homing ceiling (+06 against $F95C) and the alignment (\|diff\| against $50). Transcribe them as N of the wrapped difference, e.g. dx in [$8000,$83E7] counts as far.
- The fighters' near test is OR-shaped (&07C0A-&07C30): near if -1000 <= dx < 1000 whatever dz, or dx < -1000 and -1000 <= dz < 1000.
- The missile life is tested with ble after subq.w (signed with V); the silo countdown with bpl (0 still counts); the explosion with beq on $1F (equality).
- Shields subtract unsigned and saturate at 0 on borrow (bcc): $260 is a 0-$FFFF quantity.
- Velocity: muls.w speed by the 2.14 sine, asl.l #1, swap: hi16 = floor(speed*S/32768), arithmetic. +1C is then neg.w (-(floor), not floor(-)); +20 isn't negated; +1E = 0.
- Turning: (bearing&$FFF - heading&$FFF) asr.w 4, floor, with no wrap: across 0 it turns the long way round. Altitude homing: (player_y - y) asr.w 3 (neutron) or asr.w 2 (homing), on the wrapped 16-bit difference.
- enemy_laser projection: ext.l, asl.l by $212, divs.w by the 16-bit cam z. On overflow (\|q\| > 32767) divs leaves the dividend, so the low word of x<<7 becomes the coordinate. z == 0 is skipped explicitly (after the damage). The quotient's low word plus the centre (add.w) is used.
- angle_to_player (called by the fighters, the homing missile and the tank) divides by dx with divs.w. With dx == 0 that's a zero divide. Under EmuTOS vector 5 (&14 -> &E00BF4) is a bare RTE [measured: Hatari, d0 forced to 0 at PC &18D82]: d1 is left as ext.l(dz), the flags come back N=0 Z=1 V=0 C=0 with X kept, the next cmp overwrites them, and the game runs on. So d1.w = dz is then range-tested against ±$FF and used as the table index.
- random8 depends on the caller's d0.w: add.w D_01842,d0; add.w d0,$5C(a6). Its addx chain takes X from that add. The mine (&07B64) calls it with whatever d0 the previous slot's handler (or player_flight) left. The d0.w left at each exit: no-op/free/countdown/silo unchanged. Mine: +16 as read, or (+0A&$7FF) if +14 was 0 on entry. Fighter: random32's word if far and idle; heading&$FFF if it turned or aimed without launching; attacker type*2 if it launched. Wanderer: unchanged if frozen, (r&$F) if idle, (r2&$1F)-$10 if it turned. Neutron: (py-y) asr 3. Homing: (py-y) asr 2 if it exploded, else ext.l(+04-$12E). enemy_laser: entry d0 if blocked, type*2 if z==0, 0 after firing (moveq #0 for sfx_start; sfx_start preserves d0/d1).
- The mine handler uses the byte/word split: st.b gives $FF; +14 is a byte for the silo but a word for mines and missiles; +22/+23/+24 are bytes; +26 is a word.
- The dispatch index is asl.w #2 of the type word into a 62-entry long table. dbra over $1D8-1 is skipped entirely when $1D8 = 0.
- The free stack holds 32-bit absolute record addresses in low RAM at &94C, pushed with (a1)+ and popped with -(a1). It is unbounded: every explosion pushes. A port that keeps slot indices must map them back to compare with captures.

### Random numbers and timing

- random32 (&04692) is called once a frame by every fighter (&07C3A for SG1 near, &07C5E near, &07C78 far), even while frozen. It is called once a frame by every unfrozen wanderer (&07CBC), plus once more when it turns (&07CD2). enemy_laser calls it once (&07E9E) only when it gets as far as drawing: +22 set, not docked, not missile view, cam z != 0. So RNG consumption depends on the renderer's +22/+2C.
- random8 (&046B2) is called once by a mine explosion outside the missile view (&07B64), with the leaked d0 described under arithmetic.
- Both generators add the mouse x accumulator D_01842, so every call depends on the steering input. random32 reads the long at $5C that random8 writes.
- Before object_handlers runs, world_render has already advanced the same state: random8 when ground dots are re-seeded (&0486C, &04876, &048E2, &048EC) and random32 during the silo launch countdown (&05656). So the handlers' numbers depend on the renderer's dot culling.
- This range reads no VBL count (&1E4), frame count ($1E2) or &29C. sfx_start (effects 0 and 1 from here) is gated by &29C.l (VBL-decremented) and D_01E56 (Timer-A-decremented). Whether it starts an effect is timing-dependent, but it only writes sound state ($2D1(a6), the D_019F2-D_01A06 sound variables, the Timer A vector &134, the MFP and YM), which no logic reads.

### What it reads of the renderer's

- +22 (in the draw list) is read by mine_handler (&07AC6) and enemy_laser (&07E18). sub_057B8 sets it at &0585A, including for exploding objects, and sub_0561C clears it at &0576A, but only on its normal path.
- +23 (in the near list) is read by launch_missile (&07EFA). It is set at &05812 (not for exploding objects) and cleared at &05766.
- +2C is read by the mine (&07ACC, &07AD4, &07B3C) and +28/+2A/+2C by enemy_laser (&07E6E-&07E76). They are written at &05800-&05808 for objects in the coarse list only. Here they're read only behind +22, so they're consistent with it.
- The camera is the player's ($12E-$132 and the player's angles) in flight, but the missile's ($26A-$26E, $270-$274, &05414-&05442) in missile view. Then the mine explodes when the MISSILE comes within $600 of it, and near-list launches are relative to the missile.
- Stale results: while docked ($2CA), sub_0561C lists only &1218/&1258 and returns at &0574E without clearing the main records' +22/+23, so they keep the last flight frame's values. Every reader in this range then exits on $2CA. In missile view, world_render skips sub_0561C/sub_057B8 whenever $27C != 0 (&053BC). That covers the 13 frames after launch and the closing frames once $276 < 15. In those frames +22/+23/+2C are stale, and neither the mine test nor launch_missile checks $2C8: mines can explode and enemies can launch from stale data (enemy_laser is blocked by $2C8).
- The projection constants $1FC, $1FE, $212 and $14C are read by enemy_laser. The missile view changes $14C/$1FE during rendering and restores them before world_render returns (&054C2/&054C8).
- Objects a handler sets exploding stay in this frame's near and draw lists (built earlier), which collisions (&08416) then reads; a slot freed here (type 0) stays in them too.
- Not read here: the list arrays ($94, $98, $90), the counts $23E/$240/$23C, the camera and object matrices, projected vertices, $2F8.

### Inputs

- D_01842 (the mouse x / turn accumulator, word) is read inside random32 (&046A0) and random8 (&046B4) on every call from this range. It is the only direct input.
- The key-state array &017C2, the last key &01852, the other mouse accumulators (&01844, &01848, &0184A, &0184C) and the joystick aren't read in this range.
- Inputs arrive indirectly through flags that keys set: $2C7 (M in cheat mode, freeze), $2C8 (L, missile view), $2CA (docking), and $280/$13E through player_flight. $2BC (the monitor type) only changes the printing and the mono line.

### Corrections to st.md

- 'Work per frame' and 'Where the time goes' say 140 object handlers. object_handlers loops over $1D8(a6) records, not 140: 107 on level 1 [measured, Hatari dump; init_objects &0A7EE: layout count + 5 per tower + 10], 139 on levels 4+.
- 'Fixed addresses' calls &94C 'ten object pointers'. It is a LIFO stack of free record pointers ($9C(a6) top, $25C count): every object whose explosion finishes is pushed (&07A96), not only missiles, and enemy missile launches pop it (&07F12). It grows past ten entries, to at most &94C + 4*$1D8. [measured: 8 s into level 1 $25C was 0 and $9C &94C, all ten slots used]
- The handler table's entries 31-61 do not exactly repeat 0-30: 42 is nothing (11 is the beam), 47 and 52 are wanderers (16 and 21 are fighters), 56 is the neutron handler (25 is homing), 57 is &081D6 (26 is &081AA). The only writes of +00 found are in init_objects, &07F3E/&07F46 and &08248, so types >= 31 may never occur.
- Silo: the missile comes ready every 2,001 frames, not 2,000 (+16 counts $7D0 down to -1), and init_objects sets it ready at start.
- Mines: they warn or explode only if in the draw list (+22, so camera z >= 0 and inside the view cone), not during 'LEVEL n' ($298) and not docked. Warning zone: z in [$600,$800). Explosion: z < $600. In missile view the warning is suppressed, and the explosion doesn't hurt the player: it sets the missile's $276 = 16 and $27C = 0. Outside it, the explosion also adds a random jolt (random8 & $3E) - $20 to the roll ($280). The pyramid (2) reverses its bob; the diamond (14) flips half a turn in pitch ($40 a frame) at each end.
- Fighters: 'near means within 1,000 units in x and z' is wrong. Near is -1000 <= dx < 1000 (any z), or dx < -1000 and -1000 <= dz < 1000 (&07C0A, the same OR shape as the coarse cull). The odds: near 1/128 laser, 15/128 missile try, 112/128 turn. Far 1/128 laser, 3/128 turn (not 1/32), 4/128 missile try (1 in 32, not 1 in 16), otherwise nothing. StarGlider One near: 1/64 laser, 15/64 homing-missile try, 48/64 turn. Turns are (bearing - heading)/16 with no wrap (the long way round past 0).
- Freeze (M, $2C7): fighters stop moving and launching but still turn and fire lasers. Wanderers freeze entirely. Missiles, mines and silos ignore it.
- Wanderers: they turn 1 frame in 64 by -16..+15 (not ±16) and try to fire 3 frames in 64 (r&$3F != 0, r&$F == 0), not 1 in 16. Their velocity stays 0 until the first turn.
- Neutron missile: it also spins (+0E += $40). The type-24 object in level 4's layout has life 0 and explodes on its first frame.
- Enemy fire (&07E18) needs the attacker in the draw list (+22), not docked and not in missile view. The damage (100 on level 1, else D_0AE5E[type] = 300 for all types) is applied before the cam z == 0 test, which skips the line, flash, sound and the $C4 count. The line ends at x 96 + (r & $7F) on the view's bottom edge.
- Missile launches: type 25 needs $2D6 set (StarGlider One near &07C56, tank &08112, pyramid launcher &081CE) AND level != 1. All other launchers (ordinary fighters always) fire type 24; it is not 'type 25 from level 2'. A launch needs a free slot, the attacker in the near list (+23), not docked and enemies active; the missile view doesn't block it. Alignment is \|heading&$FFF - bearing\| < $50 with no wrap. The spawned missile takes y + D_0AF12[type], heading - $800 for D_08354 types, speed D_07FF4[level] and life D_08056[level]; the speeds fall from -350 at level 1 to -50 from level 25.
- A spawned missile inherits every field launch_missile doesn't write from the slot's last occupant, including +2E (lasers can't hurt it) when that was a walker, stomper or StarGlider One.
- Object +14/+16/+18 'timers': +14 is a byte flag for the silo but a word count for mines (the bob) and missiles (life). +16 is the silo's countdown or the mine's bob step; +18 is the mine's bob period.
- Random numbers: random8 (&046B2) also adds the caller's d0.w to $5C. At &07B64 that d0 is a value left by the previous slot's handler (or by player_flight), so a mine explosion's random number and the RNG state after it depend on register leftovers. The ground dots and the launch countdown inside world_render also consume random numbers before the handlers run.
- init_objects' tower expansion (&0A9B0, outside this range but met here): besides the two towers ((x-$100, z+$FA0) and (x+$100, z+$FA0)) and the beam (x, z+$7D0), each tower adds two type-9 tanks: (x+$5DC, z+$3E8) heading $400 and (x-$5DC, z-$3E8) heading $C00, both speed $40, +16 $64, hit points 5 + level. All of level 1's tanks come from them.

### What an exact transcription must keep

- Velocity (launch &07FB6-&07FEC, also L_07D46): vx = -(word)((speed*S << 1) >> 16) and vz = (word)((speed*C << 1) >> 16). This is muls.w, asl.l #1, swap, which floors (arithmetic) the product / 32768, and for vx neg.w is applied AFTER the floor. S = table[h&&FFF] and C = table[(h&&FFF)+1024]. No overflow is possible (\|product\| < 2^30). vy (+1E) = 0.
- Turn toward (L_07D32, Juno &08194-&081A2): h0 = +0C & &FFF; +0C = h0 + asr.w(t - h0, 4). The shift floors, and nothing takes the short way round: from 10 toward 4090 it turns the long way. The result stays inside 0..&FFF.
- Unmasked heading arithmetic: the tank adds &20 (and tests the sum & &3FF == 0), the Pyramid Launcher and &081D6 add &FFC0 and the tri-launcher &FFA0. All are 16-bit word wraps; the stored word is not reduced mod 4096. Masks are applied only where the heading is used.
- The 'near' test (tank &080E0-&08106, the same code in the fighters at &07C0A-&07C30) uses bpl/bmi on the 16-bit difference, not true signed compares, and only tests z when dx < -1000. With dx = (x - px) and dz = (z - pz) as words: near <=> dx in [-1000, 999] OR (dx in [-31768, -1001] AND dz in [-1000, 999]). So it is a strip 2000 wide running the whole z length of the torus, plus that quirk; dx in [-32768, -31769] is far because cmp.w #$3E8 overflows.
- Alignment (&07EDC): \|bearing - (heading & &FFF)\| < &50, where bearing is 0..&FFF. There is no wrap at 0/&FFF: a heading of &FFE does not align with a bearing of &002.
- Missile heading: (launcher +0C - (D_08354[type] ? &800 : 0)) & &FFF, stored masked. Missile y = launcher y + D_0AF12[type], a word wrap.
- Random tests take the low bits of random32's returned word, which is the high word of the new state: tank near &7F then 3 (on the already-masked value), tank drive &F, Juno 7, Pyramid &F, &081D6 7, type 3 &1F, beam &7FF.
- Level tests: cmpi.w #1 / beq (equality); cmpi.w #3 / bmi (sign of the difference).
- random32 (&04692): s = s*2, then EOR &1D872B41 unless (no carry and non-zero) [bhi]; then add.w (&01842) to the LOW WORD ONLY (no carry into the high word); then add.l $5C(a6); store; swap.
- The free stack is LIFO and unbounded. The record a missile gets depends on the order objects died, and if that record's index is above the current object's, the missile's own handler runs (moves it, counts down its life) in the same frame.
- angle_to_player's divs: when dx = 0 it divides by zero (the result keeps d1 = ext dz; table a at index dz + &FF, or &400/&FC00 when \|dz\| >= &FF). dz = -32768 with dx = -1 overflows. neg.w of -32768 stays negative in the \|dx\| and \|dz\| compare.

### Random numbers and timing

- random32 (&04692) call sites in the range, each conditional: tank near &08108 (1 a frame when $2C7 and near); tank driving &08160 (1 a frame when far and +16 != 0; none while turning or frozen); Juno &0817C (1 a frame when +23, even when frozen); Pyramid Launcher &081C2 (when +23 and level != 1); &081D6 &081E4 (dead); type 3 &08222 (when +22, even when frozen), plus enemy_laser's own random32 at &07E9E when it fires and passes its tests; beam &0823C (every beam every frame from level 3, anywhere in the world). The tri-launcher and launch_missile draw no randoms.
- Because the calls are conditioned on +22/+23, the random sequence depends on the renderer's lists, and those depend on the camera matrix and transform, so they have to be exact.
- random32 adds the mouse x accumulator &01842, which the ACIA interrupt updates asynchronously. A mouse packet arriving during the handler loop would change the value between two calls in the same frame.
- No VBL count (&1E4) or frame count ($1E2) is read in this range.

### What it reads of the renderer's

- +23 (near list) gates launch_missile (&07EFA), the Juno (&08174), the Pyramid Launcher (&081AA), &081D6 and the tri-launcher (&081F4).
- +22 (draw list) gates the type-3 handler (&0821A) and enemy_laser (&07E18); enemy_laser goes on to read +28/+2A/+2C, $212, $1FC, $1FE and $14C (first half).
- Both flags come from the coarse pass and sub_057B8, which world_render runs earlier in the same frame. In missile view ($2C9) the lists are built with the camera at the player's missile ($26A-$26E copied into $12E-$132, &05414-&0544A), so +22/+23 then mean relative to the missile. angle_to_player and the near test still use the player.
- While docked ($2CA) the coarse pass is skipped (&05672-&0574E builds a list of the tunnel objects only). The 140 records keep their last +22/+23, so the Juno keeps turning and the type-3 objects keep drawing randoms on stale flags. Launches and lasers are blocked by $2CA.

### Inputs

- No key-state (&017C2) or last-key (&01852) reads in this range.

### What an exact transcription must keep

- angle_to_player, the index error: T = table[(q + 255) & &1FF], but both tables hold 0 at index 256. So path A returns atan(q-1) for q = trunc(dz/dx) in -255..254, and path B returns atan((q-1)/256) for q = trunc(256*dz/dx). Path A uses whole-number ratios: dz/dx in [1,2) gives 0 (as if dz = 0) where atan2 gives 45-63 degrees, and dz/dx in (-2,-1] gives atan(-2) = -721. [measured: Hatari, Juno cannon record 47 at x &8540 z &5200, player at x &7D40 z &4600: at the rts &08414 (Hatari &18DD2), d0 = &800, d3 = &BF6, a1 = &13568 and d1 = &03F6F400. The word &F400, masked &400, is the bearing of dz = 0, where atan2 gives about &681.] The port must keep the table, the +255 and the integer ratio.
- angle_to_player, dx = 0: divs.w by zero raises vector 5. EmuTOS's vector 5 (&E00BF4) is a bare rte [measured: Hatari 'd $e00bf4'], so execution goes on at &083C6 with d1 = ext.l(dz) unchanged [measured: a breakpoint at &083C4 with d0 = 0 hit several times a frame with the player at a Juno cannon's x; at &083C6, d1 = d3 = dz = &FF6]. The 'quotient' is then dz itself: &400 for dz >= 255, &FC00 for dz <= -256, A[dz+255] otherwise. Heading 0 leaves x unchanged and objects sit on whole sectors, so dx = 0 is common in play.
- angle_to_player, signs and wrap: dx and dz are sub.w differences (the torus wrap), then ext.l. \|v\| is neg.w, so -32768 stays -32768. The path choice is cmp.w d2,d3 / blt, a signed compare that sees V: dz = -32768 takes path B for every dx except -32768, and with dx = 0 it divides by zero there too (d1 = &FF800000, low word 0, index 255, B[255] = -2).
- angle_to_player, divs overflow: the 68000 leaves the destination unchanged. Path A overflows only at dz = -32768, dx = -1 (low word &8000). Path B overflows for dz = -32768 with \|dx\| < 256 (low word 0). Only d1's low word is used afterwards; the high word keeps the remainder.
- angle_to_player, the clamps are bpl on cmp, the N flag only, not signed >=. 'cmp #&FF; bpl' counts q < -32513 as >= 255, so with dx = +1 and dz in [-32768,-32514] path A returns &400 (masked &800) where it should be 0. 'cmp #&FF01; bpl' takes q >= -255.
- angle_to_player, the result is d1 = -(base - T), word arithmetic: -&1000..0 unmasked. For dx >= 0 the base is &C00 (dx = 0 counts as positive, through ext.l/bpl).
- Box tests (collisions &08452-&08494, laser &08770-&087A8): each axis is two cmp.w tests on the N flag, (v - s) < 0 and (-v - s) < 0. That is a strict -s < v < s for normal values. v = -32768 never hits; s = 0 never hits, except in the laser when the difference is -32768 exactly. The tests are in camera space, which includes the player's pitch and roll, using positions from this frame's render (before this frame's movement). Only the first hit in list order counts.
- A ram's flag outlives the ram when a later frame's first box hit is a beam: refuel (&0851C) neither sets nor clears $2BF or $AC, so the main loop charges the rammed object's cost again (and flashes &555) every frame the player stays in the beam's box with nothing else hit first. [measured 2026-10-18, tests/st/steps_collide_ram.txt: drifting out of tower 11's box (camera z -82 to -122) into its group's beam at level 15, the shields lost 7,500 a frame for five frames.]
- Laser projection: ext.l, asl.l by $212 (7), divs.w by the camera z (1..&2000; the draw list guarantees z >= 0 and \|x\|,\|y\| <= 2z, and z = 0 is skipped), plus $1FC/$1FE. The centre can't overflow (\|x*128/z\| <= 256). The half-sizes overflow only at z = 1 for sizes >= 256 (xz 360: types 6 and 18; y 270, 390, 420: types 4, 1, 10, 15). Then divs leaves s << 7, whose low word is negative (e.g. &B400), and the object can't be hit.
- Laser timing: subq.w on $284, with the hit test on reaching 0. The decrement depends on $23E (the draw-list count) and $1E2 (the frame count) as in player_laser; the firing frame itself may decrement. With >= 6 objects drawn a held key fires every 4 frames.
- Bolt geometry (&0881C-&0888C): asr.w #2 (floor) and andi #3 on the deltas, muls.w by (4 - $284), add.w (wraps); the line runs from P to P - step. Drawing only.
- Saturating word arithmetic: energy addi.w #&190 with bcc, else &FFFF; shields subi.w #&1F40 with bcc, else 0; laser energy subi.w #&100 with bcc, else 0 (firing needs only a non-zero $28A). Counters $B0, $B8, $C0 are addq.l. Hit points are subq.w #1 then bpl: they explode below 0.
- Heading tests: add.w of the object's +C and $13C, then andi.w #&FFF, then cmp/bpl/bmi. Dock: aligned when the sum is in [0,&154) or [&EAC,&FFF], i.e. -&154..+&153 (one more unit on the negative side). Refuel: the sum in [&60C,&9F4) = &800 - &1F4 .. &800 + &1F3.
- Refuel z and x tests: cmpi with bpl/bmi (N flag), which equals a signed compare for these ranges. The z < &7D0 test is redundant inside the beam's \|z\| < 2000 box.
- eori.w #&B on the beam's type word toggles 11 and 0 (the beam handler &08248 also puts it out by writing 0, and a later tower hit brings it back).
- Pilot rating: muls.w #100 on the low word of $B8; divs.w by the low word of $B0 (skipped when 0); andi.l #&FF keeps the quotient's low byte; then muls.w level by $29E, divs.w #100, cmp.w #11/bmi (N flag), capped at 11, asl.w #2, used as a word index.
- List order matters: both lists are walked in ascending record index. The coarse list is pushed downward from $DAC(a6) at &057A4, then read upward and pushed downward again from $1214(a6) and $FE0(a6) at &05816 and &0585E, so the missile slots (records 0-9) come first, then the layout. The first match wins in collisions and the laser. In an energy-tower group, the beam (tower + 3) is tested before its two tanks (+4, +5).
- Pointers in state ($94, $98, $AC, $A8, the list entries) hold ST addresses (&5D19A + 64n, &1218, &1258). A byte-for-byte compare has to map them.

### Random numbers and timing

- No call to random32 (&04692) or random8 (&046B2) anywhere in &08390-&08CA0.

### What it reads of the renderer's

- collisions reads the near list ($98, count $240) and +28/+2A/+2C. world_render built both earlier in the same frame (&057B8; rotation &04092 by the camera matrix from the player's angles), before read_keys, player_flight and object_handlers moved anything. A record freed or reused by a handler after the render is still in the list: a freed record has type 0 and size 0, so it never hits; a reused missile slot keeps the previous occupant's camera position.

### Corrections to st.md

- 'The game: Silos': research/st.md says to dock 'with your heading within ±&154 of the silo's'. The test is on the sum: (silo +C + player $13C) & &FFF in [0,&154) or [&EAC,&FFF] (&085B4-&085CA). That is the silo's heading as drawn in camera space (world_render forms the same sum at &0513A), within -&154..+&153 of 0. Object headings run opposite to the player's.
- 'Ramming' says shields lose &0AE1E[level][type] '(1000 for silos)'. Touching a silo's box while misaligned only sets $2BF and $AC. The frame loop then flashes &555 and plays effect 1 (&020BA-&020C4) but skips the shield loss for type 6 (&020D8), so the silo entry in &0AE1E is never used. A missile flying into a silo flashes &555 the same way.
- 'Ramming': add that the box is strict (-s < v < s), in camera space (rotated by the player's pitch and roll), and uses positions from this frame's render, before this frame's movement. Only the first box hit in the near list, ascending record index, counts each frame. Types 0, 17, 22 and 28-30 have size 0 and never collide. A beam's box (2000 x 120 x 2000) is never a ram, and it hides any object later in the list while you're inside it.
- 'Refuelling' (z &79E-&7D0, heading &60C-&9F4, \|x\| < &2BC): the test runs once per contact, on the first frame inside the beam's box, latched by $2C4. The exact ranges are camera z in [&79E,&7D0), camera x in [-&2BC,&2BC) and (beam +C + $13C) & &FFF in [&60C,&9F4). The result in $2C3 then holds for the whole contact. It is cleared only by a frame with no box hit, or by docking; a frame whose first hit is another object keeps it. Sfx 8 plays once, at the passing test; the &500 flash and +&190 energy come every frame of the contact.
- 'Enemies' and 'Trig tables': angle_to_player indexes the arctan tables one entry below their centre, so it returns atan(q-1) [measured in Hatari]. The tables are centred at index 256 (0 there): A[n+256] = trunc(atan(n)), B[n+256] = trunc(atan(n/256)), both in 4096ths of a turn. Path A (\|dz\| >= \|dx\|) uses the whole-number ratio dz/dx. The research file's 'turn towards the player' therefore aims off: by up to 63 degrees when \|dz\| is 1 to 2 times \|dx\|.
- Add to the research file: at dx = 0, angle_to_player divides by zero. Under EmuTOS vector 5 is an rte, so the game survives, with the quotient = dz [measured].
- 'The player: Laser', on the countdown: research/st.md says 'The bolt takes 4 frames (counted more slowly when fewer than 6 or 3 objects are in view)'. In fact: with 6 or more drawn it steps every frame; with 3-5, only on frames where $1E2 is odd; with 0-2, on 3 frames in 4 ($1E2 & 3 != 0). So 0-2 objects is faster than 3-5. The firing frame can step too. The research file says 'two cyan bolts close from (-64, 150) and (384, 150)'. In fact four lines are drawn, from (-64,150), (-64,170), (384,150) and (384,170) ($2A8-$2AE, &08D0C-&08D1E), each a quarter-length segment, towards the sight as it was when fired ($24C/$24E), not the current sight.
- 'The player: Laser', on the hit test: it skips exploding objects, beams (type 11) and camera z = 0. It is a strict screen-space box (centre ± size << 7 / z). An object with +2 bit 7 or +2E set stops the bolt and plays effect 2 but loses no hit points and doesn't count in $B8. Shooting an energy tower from level 2 toggles its group's beam between type 11 and 0. Only the layout's own tower can do this (+18 = 1), and shooting it again restores the beam (&08894). The laser runs inside world_render, not the logic, and doesn't run in the missile view: a bolt in flight freezes there.
- 'Progression and score' and open question 6 (the pilot rating formula): research/st.md says the end screen 'rates the pilot from shots fired, hits, dockings and hits taken'. The rating is names[min(11, level * accuracy / 100)], with accuracy = (hits.w * 100) / shots.w, low byte, kept in $29E (&08A5E-&08AA6). Dockings and hits on the AGAV are printed but not used. With no shots fired the accuracy line isn't printed, and $29E keeps its previous value. The names are ROOKIE, POOR, FAIR, AVERAGE, ABOVE AVERAGE, ACCURATE, DEADLY, SUPER FLYER, ACE PILOT, COMMANDER, then CHEAT!?! for 10 and 11 (table &08C50). The research file's list leaves out five of them.
- 'The world': each energy-tower layout entry becomes three towers, a type-11 beam, and two type-9 tanks (&0A9B0-&0AA7A, read while checking +18). The tanks are at (x + &5DC, z + &3E8) heading &400 and (x - &5DC, z - &3E8) heading &C00, speed &40, +16 = &64, +30 = 5 + level. The tower expansion adds 5 to the object count.
- Object record: add +18, which on towers is 1 on the layout's own tower and marks the beam three records on (&0A9B6, read at &0889E). Note also that both lists ($94, $98) run in ascending record index.

### What an exact transcription must keep

- The score add (&00D8E): four abcd.b -(a1),-(a0), byte &01A4F into &0150F down to &01A4C into &0150C. X is clear on entry: on the colour path the last X-setter is the printer's subi.b #$20 on a digit, and rol, cmp, move and addq-to-An leave X. Measured: SR = &2300 at &0509A. The final carry is lost, so the score wraps at 10^8. The addend is the zero-extended word $D4, so the top two bytes add only the carry.
- $D4 is overwritten, never accumulated. Two kills before the next normal-view world_render score only the last. A kill during the missile view waits until the view ends. $286 takes $D4 only when $D4 != 0.
- Level change: an equality test of byte &0150D against $2D5 (beq), so any change counts, including 99 to 00 at 1,000,000; +1 a change; equal to &23 becomes &22. It runs before this frame's score add, so it fires the frame after the score crosses a 10,000 boundary.
- Order inside world_render: the last kill is printed from $286 before $286 is updated (one frame late); the score is printed after the add.
- The tunnel's end: d0 = silo.z - player.z as a word (wraps), signed cmp #$C8 / bpl. The refill is addi.w #$190 with bcc, an unsigned carry, which saturates to &FFFF. The pick-up window is signed: -&28 <= dz < &28.
- The missile view uses signed compares: cmpi #$F,$276 / bpl; cmpi #$11,$27C / bmi; $276-&F >= &38 / bpl for the blink. The bar length is 16 - ((($276-&F) asr 4) & &F), skipped if negative.
- $242 takes random32's whole low word (the swapped high word of the new state). Hardware colour 0 keeps only the ST's 9 bits, but the variable holds all 16.
- The high-score comparison is a signed cmp.l of BCD longs (a score >= 80000000 would never qualify), and strictly greater.
- $1E2 is a word that wraps; the logic tests its low bits (& &7F, & &FF, bit 2).
- Ground dots: random8 adds the caller's d0.w (and &01842) into $5C. At the first respawn of a normal-view frame (&0486C), d0 is what draw_sight left. If the sight is outside the window, or (x-14)&15 = 0, that is the zero-extended $D4. Otherwise, for d5 = (sight_x-14)&15 = 1..15 (colour): 1F00 0F80 07C0 83E0 C1F0 E0F8 F07C F83E 7C1F 3E0F 1F07 0F83 07C1 03E0 01F0 (hex). Measured: 70 of 70 frames matched the prediction at sight x 155, 160, 165 and 168.
- Exceptions in frame order: on the level-change frame the main loop skips the tail at &01F38-&0215C (energy drain, ramming damage, game-over tests) and calls frame_end a second time (&026B6). $2D7 is cleared only by a normal-view world_render. ### Random numbers and timing

- random32 (&04692) is called from &05656 on each of the 29 LAUNCHING FROM SILO frames; the result goes to $242, and the routine mixes in the mouse x accumulator &01842.
- The ground dots (&047AA, called from world_render at &050FC, and at &05436 in the missile view) call random8 twice for every dot that leaves the view cone: &0486C/&04876, or &048E2/&048EC around the missile. 153 respawns were measured in 70 flight frames.
