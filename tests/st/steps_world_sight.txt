# Flying through empty ground (x &1800, z &5000, nothing in view) with the
# turn accumulator at -8: the sight 2 pixels left of centre, on a word's
# edge, so the gunsight's blit only copies and leaves d0 as it was; a slow
# turn. Firing at nothing with nothing (or little) drawn: the bolt's count
# on three frames in four.
wait 38

wait 4
debug w w $5c02e $1800 $ffd0 $5000
debug w w $5c03c 0
debug w w $12200 $fff8
wait 0.3
debug parse {arm}
debug echo \nSEG sight\n

down 0x1c
wait 30
up 0x1c
wait 14
